Find planets hiding in public NASA data

Enter any star. The pipeline downloads every TESS sector ever observed for it, removes stellar variability, searches for repeating dips, then tries hard to prove each signal isn't a planet: odd/even depths, secondary eclipses, single-event artifacts, stellar-density consistency — and re-checks the same dip independently in every year TESS looked. Survivors are cross-matched against the NASA Exoplanet Archive to tell you whether they're already known.

  1. Download SPOC / TESS-SPOC / QLP light curves from MAST
  2. Search stacked Box-Least-Squares across observing seasons
  3. Vet 12 false-positive tests + per-season recheck
  4. Match confirmed planets & TOIs in the Exoplanet Archive
